The Pamir Topographies

Millennia-old fortress watchtowers, meteor-strike alpine lakes, and secluded high-altitude oases carved out of the Roof of the World.

EARTHQUAKE DAM · 3,263M

The Mystery of Lake Sarez

Formed overnight in February 1911 when an immense earthquake triggered a 2-billion-ton landslide blocking the Murghab River. Protected by sovereign eco-clearances.

ALEXANDER THE GREAT'S RETREAT

Iskandarkul

Legendary glacial lake named after Alexander of Macedon, bordered by the majestic Fan mountain ridges.

SHING VALLEY CASCADE

Haft Kul (Seven Lakes)

Seven distinct alpine lakes cascading down the Shing valley, each with differing mineral contents shifting colors from indigo to pale jade.

3RD CENTURY BC SILK ROAD DEFENSES

Yamchun & Silk Road Fortresses

Zoroastrian and Kushan stone fortresses overlooking the narrow Wakhan crossing between Tajikistan and Afghanistan with views of the Hindu Kush.
